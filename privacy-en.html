Privacy Policy

How your activity is used

What the Frontling research project pilot records, why, and when it is deleted. Ask us anything, any time.

Effective November 24, 2026 Operator Team PJ Contact pj.southkorea@gmail.com

At a glance

  • No names

    No name, contact details, school or email. You are known only by the participant number the operator gave you.

  • Project activity only

    Your attempts, the hints you open and what you submit are kept on your device and sent to the operator through Google Forms.

  • Your writing never goes to AI

    Hints were prepared in advance and reviewed by a person. Nothing you write is sent to an AI.

  • Deleted after a year

    Records are deleted one year after the pilot ends. Before that, you can ask to see or delete them, or withdraw consent, at any time.

Team PJ (the "operator") processes the personal information of students who use the Frontling research project pilot (the "service") as described below, in line with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. If this page and the Korean version differ, the Korean version applies.

1Why we process data

  1. Progress and feedback: so your reviewers (professors and mentors) can follow your progress and help you
  2. Improving the service: to measure which hints help and to make stuck-point suggestions more accurate
  3. Pilot results: to measure learning and how the service is used, in aggregate
  4. Your safety: so a staff member can reach out to you in person if a worrying sign is detected

Improvements and analysis use aggregate statistics and only 2-3 character fragments written by at least three different students. No sentence a student wrote is put on the public site.

What we do not process

The list matching participant numbers to names is kept by the operator, separately from the service. Please don't write anything that identifies you, such as your name or contact details, in attempts or submissions.

Google (when records are sent) and GitHub (when the site is opened) may process connection data such as your IP address and browser details (see section 5).

3How long we keep it

  • Records in Google Forms and Sheets: kept for one year (365 days) after the pilot ends, then destroyed.
  • Records on your device: kept in browser storage and removed when you clear your browser data. A file you make with "Save my record" is yours to manage.
  • How we destroy it: when the period ends or you withdraw consent, we permanently delete the response sheet and any exported files within 5 days, and shred paper consent forms.
  • Aggregate statistics and fragment weights used for analysis cannot identify anyone and may be kept after that period.

4Sharing with third parties

The operator does not give personal information to third parties, except where the law requires it.

6Children under 14

For children under 14 we process personal information with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. In this pilot we collect written consent from both the student and a guardian, whatever the student's age, and issue a participant number only after receiving it. Guardians can use the rights in section 7 too.

7Your rights and how to use them

  • Students and guardians can at any time ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of their records, or withdraw consent.
  • How: email the address in section 10 with your participant number and your request. We confirm that you are the student or a guardian and handle it within 10 days, then tell you the result.
  • If you withdraw consent, we delete that participant number's records from the response sheet and stop receiving new ones. Records on your device are removed when you clear your browser data.
  • You have the right not to consent. Without consent you cannot take part in the pilot.

8Cookies and device storage

We use no cookies and no third-party analytics or advertising tools. To pick up where you left off, the site uses browser storage (localStorage) and a cache for offline use; this stays on your device.

9How we keep it safe

  • Participant numbers instead of names; the list matching them to names is kept separately.
  • Link sharing is off on the response sheet and only the operator and reviewers can open it.
  • Everything is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the site's Content Security Policy blocks outside scripts.
  • The text of a worrying expression is never stored or sent.
